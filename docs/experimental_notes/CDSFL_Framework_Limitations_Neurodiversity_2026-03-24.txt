CDSFL Framework Limitations and Neurodiversity
24 March 2026

A LIMITATION DISCOVERED BY SELF APPLICATION

During analysis of the cognitive curves framework, the founder applied the D, v bar, A, C fingerprint to his own cognitive performance across the two week history of the CDSFL project. The result revealed a genuine limitation of the framework.

The decay curve measures one specific thing. How quickly an analytical mind exhausts a fixed problem space. It does this well. Codex 5.3 producing a curve of 5, 3, 2, 2, 0 on a proof review is a clean measurement of that specific cognitive process.

But the founder's cognitive pattern across this project was fundamentally different. It showed ascending abstraction, not decay. Week one produced many small practical fixes at the infrastructure level. API key issues, model selection, timeout bugs, the Sonnet confound. High frequency, narrow scope. Week two produced fewer but more consequential interventions at the methodology level. The HIL overpowering discovery, the confer in Control confound, the inverse square root observation, the Registry architecture. This session produced the fewest but most far reaching contributions at the theory level. The cognitive curves framework itself, the bidirectional feedback loop, the trust score integration, the ethical framework.

The finding rate decreased but the finding significance increased monotonically. This is not what the decay model measures. The framework measures exhaustion of a fixed error space. The founder was not exhausting an error space. He was expanding it. Each insight did not find another error in the same proof. It reframed what the proof was about. The decay curve cannot capture this because there is nothing decaying. The problem is growing and the cognition is growing with it.


THE NEURODIVERSITY CONNECTION

The founder is formally diagnosed autistic and dyslexic. Not in the fashionable sense where the label is self applied for social currency, but formally diagnosed and self recognised through a lifetime of experience.

This is relevant because the cognitive pattern observed, ascending abstraction, offline incubation, pattern recognition before articulation, connecting apparently unrelated domains, reflects characteristics often associated with autistic cognition. Deep systematic processing that operates on structure and relationships rather than surface features. An ability to hold complex systems in mind and see connections that neurotypical pattern matching may miss.

The founder spotted the inverse square root law from raw numbers before any formal analysis. He smelled smoke in the experimental design while watching the test run, before any model produced diagnostic data. He connected the Windows Registry concept to Group Policy to cognitive curves to Genesis trust scores in a single conversational thread. These are structural observations, not surface observations. They reflect a cognitive architecture that processes at a level of relationships and systems rather than individual data points.

The decay curve framework would classify this pattern as non convergent or non monotone. Under the framework's own logic, that is a flag for potential churn. But the apparent churn produced the framework itself. The tool misclassifies its own creator. That is the limitation, stated plainly.


WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE FRAMEWORK

The D, v bar, A, C fingerprint measures analytical capability on bounded tasks. It does not measure creative synthesis, theoretical abstraction, or cross domain insight. These are different cognitive contributions, both valuable, and the framework only captures one.

This is not a flaw that can be fixed by adding parameters. It is a boundary condition. The framework does what it does. What it does not do should be stated, not hidden.

Any serious deployment of cognitive curve measurement must acknowledge this boundary. Analytical exhaustion of a known problem space is one valid form of cognition. Creative expansion of the problem space is another. The decay curve measures the first. Nothing in the current framework measures the second. Both matter.


WHAT THIS MEANS FOR NEURODIVERSITY

The value of neurodivergent cognition may be ultimately unquantifiable. Not because it is not real. It demonstrably is. But because the instruments of measurement are designed by and for neurotypical cognitive patterns. A framework built to measure analytical exhaustion will naturally reward the cognitive style that exhausts analytically. A framework built to measure creative synthesis would reward a different style. Neither captures the full picture. Both are partial.

The ethical framework outlined in the cognitive curves analysis states that no one should be penalised for an unusual curve shape that produces good outcomes. This addresses the issue at the policy level. But the deeper point is that some cognitive contributions do not produce measurable outcomes at all until much later. The ascending abstraction pattern observed in the founder's cognition produced no measurable finding in any single round of the bench test. It produced the framework, the experimental design, the Registry architecture, and the cognitive curves concept itself, over days and weeks, not minutes and rounds. A per round measurement will always miss this kind of contribution.

This has implications beyond this project. Any system that measures cognitive patterns will systematically favour neurotypical patterns unless it explicitly accounts for the value of neurodivergent cognition. But accounting for it requires measuring it. And measuring it may require instruments that do not yet exist and may never fully capture what makes neurodivergent thinking valuable.

The honest conclusion is that the framework has a known boundary. It measures what it measures. It does not measure everything that matters. The cognitive style that created the framework falls outside the framework's measurement capability. This should be documented as a limitation, not papered over as a feature.


A NOTE ON MOTIVATION

The founder offered himself as the first test subject of his own framework knowing the framework would find him non convergent by its own measure. He did so not from ego but from the same principle that drives the entire project. Everything should be open to study. Including the person proposing the study.

Whether others choose to examine this data after the founder is gone is their decision. The observation is documented honestly. The limitation is genuine. The cognitive pattern is real and directly produced the theoretical contributions that define this project. That the framework cannot fully measure the cognition that produced it is itself a finding worth recording.

Humility is what has been beaten into me throughout my life, the founder noted. It is also what helps me to perhaps see some things more clearly than others. Without ego, everything becomes open to study. Including me.
