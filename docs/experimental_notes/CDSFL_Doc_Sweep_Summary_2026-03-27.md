# CDSFL Documentation Sweep Summary

**27 March 2026**

---

## What Was Done

After completing the meta-test and three-model confer on the CDSFL mathematical model, a full documentation sweep was carried out across the project. Five documents were updated, plus the README and the recovery resources. The goal was to ensure that everything discovered today is properly recorded, with the full meaning and significance preserved in the right places.

---

## The Documents Updated

**Experimental results file** — received two new formal experiment entries. Experiment 8 covers the five-model blind pass on the mathematical model, which found eleven genuine errors and corrected them. Experiment 9 covers the three-model confer that resolved five deferred design decisions and added a new component. Both entries include full methodology, raw data references, results tables, and caveats. The weak player compensation observation is documented as a technical finding with explicit methodological caveats.

**Founder's notes** — received a new section covering the full meta-test. This includes the two-stage structure, the specific errors that would have survived without the framework-guided models, and the communication protocol observation. The section is written in the founder's voice, as personal observation rather than technical report.

**White paper** — received two updates. Part Ten now includes a validation progress paragraph describing what was tested and what was found. Part Fourteen received a new subsection on weak player compensation, including the communication protocol hypothesis, where compensation breaks down, methodological caveats, and four testable predictions.

**Extended rationale** — received a substantial new section on the meta-test and the communication protocol observation, written for a general audience. This covers what the method found when applied to itself, what the structured output format made possible for the non-framework manager, and what the observation implies for mixed teams of humans and AI models.

**README** — received a new section between the multi-architecture review discussion and the repository contents listing. This walks the reader naturally from the biodiversity claim into the meta-test results and the communication protocol observation, with links to the experimental data, the white paper, the founder's notes, and the extended rationale.

**Onboarding and recovery files** — both updated to reflect the documentation sweep completion and the next planned work.

---

## What Was Not Done

The persistence and cryptographic layer described in Part Five of the white paper has not been built or tested. It is specified as architecture but exists only as a design. This was left as implicit per the founder's instruction. It will be built next using the same full distributed compute model that was used for the meta-test.

---

## Why This Matters

Before this sweep, the meta-test findings existed only in commit messages, log files, and accessibility notes. None of the project's canonical documentation reflected what had been discovered. The experimental results file had no entries for either the blind pass or the confer. The founder's notes had no mention of today's tests. The white paper's validation sections were written before any validation had occurred. The README made no mention of empirical results.

After this sweep, every canonical document in the project reflects the current state of the evidence. A reader encountering the project for the first time will see not just the theoretical framework but the fact that it has been applied to itself, what it found, and what the limitations of those findings are.

The weak player compensation observation — which may be the most consequential discovery of the day — is now documented as a falsifiable prediction across all relevant documents, with appropriate scope and caveats in each.
