# Final statistical review of the proposed validation plan

Reviewed: `outputs/CDSFL_revised_model_validation_plan.md`, version 1.0 proposal, 9 September 2026.

Result: **No new material statistical issues found in this read.**

The plan appropriately distinguishes the diagnostic pilot from a powered evaluation; confines calibration to independently resolvable scoped outcomes; separates observation, action, and verification; handles enriched repair cohorts with population and selection weights; preserves family-level dependence; and leaves deployment calibration, practical benefit, transfer, and originality unestablished. The stated zero-failure binomial bound includes its independence and population conditions. Sequential inspection, missing outcomes, reused evidence, and unknown alternatives are addressed without claiming more than the proposed measurements could establish.

This was a final read of the validation plan only. It does not independently establish that the companion specification's structural checks passed, and it does not provide empirical validation of the model.
