CATEGORY 3. OVERCLAIMS ABOUT EXTERNAL VALIDATION

Finding 7.
File. PAPER.md, line 10 (Abstract).
Text. Empirical third-party validation has been conducted: three independent vendor models (Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI GPT) reviewed the methodology and its implementation under shared falsification protocol...
Problem. This claims "empirical third-party validation" and describes the models as "three independent vendor models." These are not independent third parties. They are AI models operated by the same founder under the same direction. Different vendor models working together under one person's orchestration is multi-model review, not third-party validation. Third-party validation requires an independent party with no involvement in the project. The framework has NOT been externally validated.
Suggested fix. Replace "Empirical third-party validation has been conducted" with something like "Multi-model adversarial review has been conducted internally." Replace "three independent vendor models" with "five models from four vendors." Add a qualifier that this is internal review, not external validation.

Finding 8.
File. FOUNDERS_NOTES.md, line 23.
Text. ...each iteration had external validation (testbench results, real-world application).
Problem. Testbench results and real-world application by the same founder are not "external validation." External validation requires an independent party. This is self-testing and self-application.
Suggested fix. Replace "external validation" with "empirical feedback" or "empirical testing" or "testable outcomes." The distinction matters: the testbench results are empirical data, not external validation.

Finding 9.
File. FOUNDERS_NOTES.md, line 133.
Text. ...distinguished from circular reasoning by external validation at each iteration.
Problem. Same issue. The "external validation" referenced here is the testbench and real-world application, both conducted by the same practitioner. This is empirical testing, not external validation.
Suggested fix. Replace "external validation" with "empirical testing against external outcomes."

Finding 10.
File. PAPER.md, line 10 (Abstract, continued).
Text. ...identifying 44 issues across 18 review rounds, with heterogeneous architectures finding defects that homogeneous review missed.
Problem. While factually accurate about what happened, the framing as "third-party validation" in the preceding sentence makes this read as if an independent party confirmed the methodology works. The 44 issues were found during internal development, not during external review.
Suggested fix. This text is fine on its own. The fix is to correct the framing sentence before it (Finding 7).
