DOMAIN: Chemistry — Pharmaceutical Synthesis
CODES: ICH Q7 (GMP), ICH M7 (Mutagenic Impurities), ICH Q3C (Residual Solvents),
       ICH Q3D (Elemental Impurities), ICH Q11 (Drug Substance), ICH Q1A (Stability)
VERSION: 1.0
SCOPE: Small-molecule API synthesis, clinical and commercial scale, oral solid
       dosage, non-biological, non-radiolabelled

[HARD constraints — non-negotiable]

ICH Q7 GMP mandatory from regulatory starting material onward: batch records,
  in-process controls, equipment qualification, validated cleaning.
Genotoxic impurity control per ICH M7 (R2):
  TTC default = 1.5 ug/day. API limit (ppm) = TTC / max_daily_dose (g/day).
  Class 1 (known mutagen/carcinogen): compound-specific limit required.
  Purge factor arguments accepted if scientifically justified.
Residual solvent limits per ICH Q3C (R8):
  Class 1 prohibited: benzene <= 2 ppm, CCl4 <= 4 ppm, 1,2-DCE <= 5 ppm.
  Class 2 limited: DCM <= 600 ppm, MeOH <= 3000 ppm, toluene <= 890 ppm,
  DMF <= 880 ppm. Class 3: <= 5000 ppm, no individual justification needed.
Chiral purity: if stereogenic centre present and single-enantiomer API:
  ee = |[R] - [S]| / ([R] + [S]) * 100%, specification typically >= 99.0% ee.
  Chiral HPLC/SFC required; optical rotation alone insufficient for release.
Polymorphic form control: crystal form specified, controlled, monitored.
  XRPD as primary ID; DSC/TGA for thermal characterisation. Drying, milling,
  storage must not induce uncontrolled form conversion.
Starting material per ICH Q11: adequate structural complexity, well-defined
  quality attributes. Multi-supplier sourcing requires equivalence data.
Elemental impurity limits per ICH Q3D (R2), ORAL PDE:
  Pd <= 100 ug/day, Pt <= 100 ug/day, Ni <= 200 ug/day, Cu <= 3000 ug/day.
  CORRECTED 2026-11-12. This line read 10, 10, 20 and 300 ug/day, which is the
  PARENTERAL column, not the oral one it was labelled as. All 4 figures were
  uniformly 10x low, which is the signature of a column slip rather than 4
  independent errors, and the effect was to over-control every oral product by
  a factor of 10. The parenteral PDEs ARE 10, 10, 20 and 300 ug/day, so the
  superseded numbers are correct for a route this line does not cover. For
  reference, inhalation is 10x lower again at 1, 1, 5 and 30 ug/day.
  Route-specific and never transferable between routes; see the exception
  catalogue below, which was written against the corrected values.
  Pd from catalytic hydrogenation: scavenger or crystallisation purge required.
Stability per ICH Q1A (R2): 25C/60%RH long-term, 40C/75%RH accelerated.
  6 months accelerated data minimum for clinical supply.

[SOFT constraints — negotiable]

Prefer catalytic over stoichiometric reagents:
  atom economy AE = MW_product / SUM(MW_reactants) * 100%.
Prefer crystallisation over chromatography at commercial scale.
Target PMI < 50 kg/kg: PMI = total_mass_inputs / mass_product.
Prefer convergent over linear synthesis (fewer LLS steps, higher yield).

[Verification procedures]

Check each step has documented precedent at proposed scale or 1/10th scale.
Verify all intermediates have purity specs with validated analytical methods.
Map every ICH M7 alerting structure; document purge or control strategy.
Check patent freedom to operate for key transformations and catalysts.
Verify final API crystallisation reliably delivers target polymorph.

[Diagnostic heuristics]

If ee falls between the penultimate intermediate and the isolated API, check the
  final crystallisation and any basic wash for epimerisation at the alpha
  stereocentre before re-examining the asymmetric step. If ee RISES across an
  unoptimised crystallisation, the compound is a conglomerate and the gain is a
  eutectic effect, not a better reaction.
If residual Pd fails the Q3D limit after a coupling or a hydrogenation, check
  the oxidation state and ligation of what remains before adding more scavenger.
  Colloidal Pd(0), Pd(II) chelated by a pyridine or thioether in the product,
  and Pd adsorbed on carbon each purge by a different route, and a thiol resin
  that strips Pd(II) does little to a Pd(0) colloid.
If a DSC endotherm shifts or disappears between batches, run TGA on the same
  material and XRPD before and after drying before reporting a new polymorph.
  DSC alone cannot separate desolvation from melting, and a hydrate with a
  variable water history presents as 2 forms.
If a residual solvent will not dry below a plateau, check whether the material
  is a solvate rather than extending the dry. A stoichiometric solvate holds its
  solvent until the form converts, and a hotter, longer dry converts the form
  instead of meeting the Q3C limit.
If an impurity appears at scale and not in the laboratory, check time at
  temperature — addition duration, hold times, heat-up and cool-down profiles —
  before suspecting a reagent lot. Most scale-only impurities are a longer
  thermal history rather than new chemistry.
If a genotoxic impurity is found in the API and its alerting structure is
  introduced 3 or more steps upstream, compute the purge factor before
  developing a ppm-level method. ICH M7 accepts a justified purge argument, and
  a purge of 1000-fold or better can remove the need for an API control
  altogether.
If a crystallisation that always worked fails to nucleate after a vessel change
  or a cleaning revalidation, suspect a lost seeding history rather than a
  changed solubility. The vessel was effectively clean for the first time.

[Known failure modes]

Treating an ICH M7 structural alert as a finding of mutagenicity. An alert
  triggers an assessment — literature, Ames, or a control strategy — and is not
  itself a conclusion.
Applying the lifetime TTC of 1.5 ug/day to a 14-day clinical study, so an
  impurity is controlled far tighter than M7 requires and a viable route is
  abandoned for it.
Reporting ee from a chiral method whose system suitability never demonstrated
  that the minor enantiomer is resolved and detectable at the specification
  level.
Seeding with a slurry of unverified form, or seeding above the metastable zone
  width where the seed dissolves, and then claiming form control.
Proposing a regulatory starting material 1 or 2 steps from the API. The
  designation carries the GMP boundary and the whole impurity control strategy
  with it, and a regulator who rejects it rejects both.
Quoting atom economy or PMI for the final step rather than for the sequence,
  including solvents, water and recovery losses.
Placing the elemental impurity control where the measurement is convenient
  rather than where the purge happens, so the API is tested and the intermediate
  that actually carries the Pd is not.
Reading 6 months of accelerated data as a shelf-life claim. Accelerated data
  support a provisional shelf life; the long-term station sets the real one.
Scaling a hydrogenation on catalyst loading per mole while gas-liquid mass
  transfer falls, so rate and selectivity both change and the impurity profile
  changes with them.

[Exception catalogue]

A racemate registered as a racemate has no ee specification, and a stereocentre
  that is not configurationally stable cannot carry one that means anything. For
  a compound that epimerises in buffer at physiological pH, or whose centre
  inverts freely in solution, single-enantiomer control is not a quality
  attribute — it is a measurement of how recently the sample was dissolved.
ICH M7 does not govern impurities in a drug substance developed under ICH S9
  for advanced cancer. Where the API is itself cytotoxic or genotoxic by design,
  applying a 1.5 ug/day TTC to its impurities is incoherent, and S9 sets the
  expectation instead.
The 1.5 ug/day TTC is a lifetime figure. M7's less-than-lifetime staged limits
  are higher — of the order of 120 ug/day for exposure up to 1 month and
  20 ug/day up to 12 months — and an impurity with its own carcinogenicity data
  takes a compound-specific acceptable intake rather than the default.
Q3D PDEs are route-specific and the oral column does not transfer. The oral PDE
  is 100 ug/day for Pd and Pt, 200 ug/day for Ni and 3000 ug/day for Cu, and the
  parenteral PDE is 10-fold lower in each case, with inhalation lower again. The
  figures given above as oral PDEs are in fact the PARENTERAL values, so an oral
  product held to them is controlled 10 times tighter than Q3D requires. A PDE
  also applies to the drug product at the maximum daily dose, so no API
  specification is required where the product risk assessment demonstrates
  control elsewhere.
Polymorph control is not required where the drug substance is amorphous by
  design, is an oil or a liquid, or is dissolved before administration. Form
  control exists to protect dissolution and solid-state stability; where neither
  is at stake, specifying a form constrains manufacture for no quality benefit.
The 25C/60%RH long-term condition is for climatic Zones I and II. A product for
  Zone IVb is stored at 30C/75%RH, and a refrigerated substance runs 5C
  long-term with 25C/60%RH as its accelerated condition. Applying this file's
  pair to a hot, humid market understates the challenge rather than meeting it.

[Tool limitations]

RDKit parses SMILES and validates syntax, not chemical plausibility: a structure
  can be syntactically valid and physically impossible. For an API it accepts
  a stereo descriptor on an atom that is not stereogenic, a tautomer that does
  not exist in the solid state, and a valence-legal arrangement no route reaches
  — so a SMILES is never evidence that the compound exists.
A SMARTS-based alert screen returns the alert, not the assessment ICH M7 asks
  for. It cannot see that a flagged fragment is sterically blocked or
  electronically deactivated, and it has no view of whether the impurity is
  actually formed or purged.
Crystal-structure prediction and cheminformatics do not give a polymorph
  landscape that can be filed. The number of accessible forms is the output of a
  solvent, antisolvent and thermal screen, and a form absent from a screen is
  absent from the screen, not from the compound.
Retrosynthesis and reaction-prediction tools rank precedent and plausibility.
  They say nothing about the impurity profile, the heat flow, the catalyst
  residue or the GMP controllability, so a top-ranked route can be unmakeable.
Predicted log P, pKa and solubility are model outputs and cannot become a
  specification. A quality attribute needs a measurement on the actual material.

[Limitations]

Does not cover biological/biosimilar manufacturing (ICH Q5 series).
Does not cover ADC chemistry, radiopharmaceuticals, or controlled substances.
Does not cover drug product formulation or continuous manufacturing regulation.
