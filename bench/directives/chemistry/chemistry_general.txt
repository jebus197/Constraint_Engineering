SEVERITY SCALE: GHS health hazard category, critical at Category 2.
       ★ THE SCALE IS INVERTED: Category 1 is the MOST severe, so a higher
       number is LESS severe and any ordering code assuming otherwise inverts
       the meaning. Declaring Category 2 makes Categories 1 and 2 critical.
       Acute toxicity sorts into 5 categories, not 4.
SOURCES: UN GHS. Rev. 10 is ST/SG/AC.10/30/Rev.10, published 2023-07-27 by

       are deliberately not stated here rather than guessed.

★ WHY THIS ENCODING EXISTS. The March 2026 analysis found all 3 chemistry
tasks were given the ANALYTICAL chemistry box because it sorts first
alphabetically, and none was analytical chemistry: one needed
electrochemistry, one pharmaceutical crystallisation, one process
distillation. This is the general box that was missing, and it is the declared
default.

[HARD constraints — non-negotiable]

Mass balance: atoms are conserved. Every element must balance on both sides of
  every equation, and an unbalanced equation is not an approximation.

Charge balance: net charge is conserved in every reaction, including every
  half-reaction. An electrochemical result with unbalanced charge has an error
  upstream of its potential.

Moles are not mass and not volume. Stoichiometry runs on moles, and a ratio
  applied to masses without the molar masses is the commonest error in the
  domain.

Limiting reagent must be identified before any yield claim. A yield computed
  against the wrong reagent exceeds 100 per cent or understates silently.

Equilibrium constants are dimensionless by convention with a stated standard
  state. Mixing concentration-based and pressure-based constants without
  conversion is an error that survives a units check.

Thermodynamic favourability is not kinetic accessibility. A negative free
  energy says nothing about rate, and conflating the 2 predicts reactions that
  do not occur on any useful timescale.

Significant figures must reflect the least precise input, and a pH is a
  logarithm so its significant figures live in the mantissa only.

Temperature must be absolute wherever it appears other than as a difference,
  for the same reason as in thermodynamics.

[SOFT constraints — negotiable]

Prefer explicit half-reactions for any redox process.
State the solvent and the ionic strength; activity coefficients are not 1 in
  real solutions and the deviation grows with concentration.
Prefer reporting a reaction's conditions alongside its outcome.
State whether a quantity is per mole of reaction or per mole of a named
  species.
Prefer standard enthalpies with their reference state named.

[Verification procedures]

rdkit for any structural claim, SMILES validity and molecular formula; never
  count atoms by hand.
Balance every equation by execution -- construct the element count on both
  sides and compare -- rather than by inspection.
pint for every concentration, rate and energy expression.
scipy for equilibrium and kinetic solutions, with the mass balance checked on
  the computed answer.
Check a computed pH against the bounds 0 to 14 only as a sanity heuristic, not
  a constraint, because concentrated solutions legitimately exceed them.

[Diagnostic heuristics]

If a yield exceeds 100 per cent, the limiting reagent or the molar mass is
  wrong.
If an equation balances for every element except hydrogen or oxygen, water or
  protons are missing from an aqueous half-reaction.
If an equilibrium calculation gives a negative concentration, the assumed
  direction was wrong.
If a rate constant has unexpected units, the reaction order is wrong.
If a thermochemical result is out by a factor near 1000, a kilojoule and a
  joule have been mixed.

[Known failure modes]

Applying a mole ratio to masses.
Unbalanced charge in a half-reaction.
Mixing concentration and pressure equilibrium constants.
Assuming activity equals concentration at high ionic strength.
Confusing thermodynamic and kinetic feasibility.
Celsius where kelvin is required.
Assuming a higher GHS category number means greater hazard.

[Exception catalogue]

pH below 0 and above 14 is real in concentrated acids and bases, so a
  plausibility check on the 0 to 14 range will refuse correct work.
Non-aqueous and molten-salt systems have different solvent constraints
  entirely.
Some reactions are legitimately unbalanced as written because they are net
  equations summarising a sequence; the sequence must then be given.
Catalytic cycles consume no catalyst overall, so a mass balance on the
  catalyst correctly nets to zero.
Isotopic labelling makes 2 chemically identical species distinguishable, and a
  formula-based comparison will merge them.

[Tool limitations]

rdkit validates structure and not synthesisability or stability.
pint cannot distinguish molar from specific quantities, nor energy from
  torque, because each pair shares dimensions.
Equilibrium solvers return a mathematical root that may be physically
  inadmissible -- a negative concentration -- without flagging it.
Thermochemical databases are method- and state-specific, and values from
  different sources are not interchangeable in the last figures.
