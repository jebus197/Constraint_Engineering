[HARD constraints — non-negotiable]

Biological variation is the signal, not noise to be averaged away. A single
  measurement on a single organism supports no population claim, and the n and
  the sampling frame must be stated.

Correlation in observational biology is not causation, and the confounders in
  living systems are numerous and usually unmeasured. A causal claim needs an
  intervention or an explicit identification argument.

Units and scale must be stated: per organism, per gram, per unit area, per
  unit time. A rate without its denominator is the commonest error here and it
  balances dimensionally often enough to survive a units check.

Taxonomic names must be current and must carry their authority where ambiguity
  exists. A synonym treated as a distinct taxon inflates diversity counts.

Evolutionary arguments must not be teleological. A trait does not exist in
  order to serve a function; selection acts on variation that already exists,
  and the reversed framing produces unfalsifiable claims.

Homeostasis bounds: physiological quantities have ranges outside which the
  organism is not functioning rather than merely unusual. A model producing a
  value outside the range has an error, and the range locates it.

Sample independence must be established. Individuals from one site, one clutch
  or one clone are not independent, and treating them as such understates the
  interval exactly as autocorrelation does in time series.

[SOFT constraints — negotiable]

Prefer reporting the distribution over the mean for any biological quantity.
Prefer effect sizes with intervals over significance tests.
State the life stage, sex and condition of organisms; most physiological
  quantities depend on all 3.
Prefer a mechanistic explanation that makes a further prediction over one that
  merely fits.
State whether a measurement is in vivo, in vitro or in silico; the 3 are not
  interchangeable evidence.

[Verification procedures]

Biopython for any sequence handling rather than string manipulation.
scipy.stats and statsmodels for the inference, with every proportion carrying
  an interval and the method named.
pint for any rate or concentration expression.
Check the physiological plausibility of every computed value against its known
  range, by execution against a stated range rather than by recall.
For any count or diversity measure, report the sampling effort alongside;
  diversity measures are strongly effort-dependent.

[Diagnostic heuristics]

If a physiological value is an order of magnitude from the known range, check
  the units before the biology -- per litre against per millilitre is the
  usual cause.
If a diversity measure rises with sampling effort, it has not saturated and
  the comparison between sites is confounded by effort.
If a dose-response curve is monotone over the whole range, the range is too
  narrow to see the turnover.
If a trait correlates with body size, check whether the relationship survives
  normalising for it; most do not.
If an effect is present in vitro and absent in vivo, the in vitro condition is
  the finding.

[Known failure modes]

Pseudoreplication: treating non-independent samples as independent.
Reporting a rate without its denominator.
Using an outdated taxonomic name and double-counting a taxon.
Teleological reasoning about adaptation.
Extrapolating a dose-response relationship beyond the tested range.
Ignoring allometric scaling when comparing across body sizes.
Treating a laboratory strain's physiology as representative of the wild type.

[Exception catalogue]

Clonal and inbred populations legitimately have near-zero genetic variance, so
  a low-variance result there is correct rather than suspicious.
Some physiological extremes are real: hibernation, diapause and diving
  responses produce values far outside the active range and a plausibility
  check calibrated on the active range will refuse them.
Non-model organisms may lack reference ranges entirely, and the honest report
  is that no range exists rather than borrowing one.
Phylogenetic non-independence means species are not independent data points,
  so a comparative analysis needs a phylogenetic correction.

[Tool limitations]

Sequence tools validate syntax and not biological plausibility; a valid
  sequence may be nonsense.
Statistical libraries assume independence and will give an answer for
  pseudoreplicated data without complaint.
Reference ranges in any database are population- and method-specific, and
  applying one across populations is an assumption that must be stated.
No tool distinguishes a biologically meaningful effect from a statistically
  detectable one.
