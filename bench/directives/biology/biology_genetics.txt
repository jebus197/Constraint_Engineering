SEVERITY SCALE: CTCAE v5.0 grade, critical at Grade 3, where the work bears on
       a person. Research-only work inherits the application's scale.
SOURCES: NCI CTEP CTCAE v5.0 (2017), confirmed current 2026-11-12.

[HARD constraints — non-negotiable]

Coordinates require a genome build. A position without its assembly is
  meaningless, and the same number refers to different loci across builds.
  This is the commonest source of a silently wrong variant call.

Strand must be stated. A sequence and its reverse complement are different
  strings and a variant reported on the wrong strand is a different variant.

Reading frame must be stated for any coding interpretation. An indel whose
  length is not a multiple of 3 shifts the frame, and the consequence is
  qualitatively different from a substitution.

Reference and alternate alleles must not be swapped. The swap inverts every
  downstream frequency and zygosity claim while remaining internally
  consistent.

Multiple testing across the genome is severe. A nominally significant
  association at one of millions of positions is expected by chance, and the
  correction must be stated.

Hardy-Weinberg expectations are a diagnostic, not an assumption. Departure
  usually indicates a genotyping artefact before it indicates selection.

Variant interpretation must distinguish EVIDENCE from CONCLUSION. A variant of
  uncertain significance is a result, and forcing it into pathogenic or benign
  is a fabrication of certainty.

[SOFT constraints — negotiable]

Prefer standard nomenclature for variants, with the transcript identifier
  included.
Prefer reporting allele frequency with the population it came from; frequencies
  are strongly population-specific.
State the sequencing depth and the caller for any variant claim.
Prefer a functional assay over a prediction where the decision matters.
State whether expression is absolute or relative, and relative to what.

[Verification procedures]

Biopython for sequence manipulation, translation and reverse complement --
  never by hand, because a hand-written reverse complement is a transcription
  risk with no upside.
Round-trip every coordinate conversion and compare.
Translate the coding sequence by execution and check the protein against the
  expected length and the stop position.
scipy.stats and statsmodels for association statistics, with the multiple-
  testing correction named.
Check Hardy-Weinberg by execution as a genotyping diagnostic.

[Diagnostic heuristics]

If a variant looks novel and the position is suspiciously round, check the
  build.
If a protein comes out the wrong length, check the frame before the sequence.
If allele frequencies sum to something other than 1, the alleles are
  mis-assigned.
If an association signal is a single isolated position with no neighbours, it
  is probably an artefact; real signals have linkage structure around them.
If expression changes are uniformly in one direction, suspect a normalisation
  problem.

[Known failure modes]

Build mismatch between coordinate systems.
Strand and reverse-complement errors.
Reference and alternate allele swap.
Frame errors on indels.
Treating a prediction tool's score as a functional result.
Ignoring population structure in an association study.
Reporting a variant of uncertain significance as pathogenic or benign.

[Exception catalogue]

Mitochondrial and chloroplast genomes have their own genetic codes, so the
  standard translation table is wrong for them.
Selenocysteine and pyrrolysine recode stop codons, so an apparent premature
  stop may be correct.
RNA editing means the transcript sequence legitimately differs from the
  genomic one.
Polyploid and aneuploid organisms break diploid zygosity assumptions entirely.
Some regions are legitimately unmappable, and a gap there is honest rather
  than a failure.

[Tool limitations]

Variant callers have systematic biases by variant type and region, and a
  single caller's output is a single route.
Prediction tools are trained on known variants and are optimistic on anything
  resembling their training set.
Annotation databases disagree with one another, and the disagreement is not
  noise but a difference in evidence standards.
Biopython validates syntax and not biological plausibility.

[Limitations]

Does not cover clinical reporting or regulatory requirements.
Does not cover structural biology or protein folding.
Does not cover population genetics theory in depth.
Does not cover laboratory protocol validation.
