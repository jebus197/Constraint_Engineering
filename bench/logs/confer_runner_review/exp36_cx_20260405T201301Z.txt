**Assessment**
The runner is not fit for live execution in its current form. The star/blackboard shape is present, and several past mistakes are avoided: FFF is prompt-only, Gemini nested-dict findings are handled, float stringification for Merkle sealing is present, `resolve()` is live, and `has_follow` dead metadata appears gone.

The blocking problems are structural: merge handling is inverted, the convergence gate is weaker than specified, verdict-only rounds can manufacture bogus findings, resume breaks canonical state, and gamma is not the stated Duane metric. I could not verify “undiscussed prompt inventions” from code alone, so I am not counting that as a finding.

Definitive stance: this runner should not be used live until F036-001 through F036-005 are fixed; F036-006 and F036-007 should be cleaned up in the same pass to make the status model and prompt/protocol contract internally consistent.
