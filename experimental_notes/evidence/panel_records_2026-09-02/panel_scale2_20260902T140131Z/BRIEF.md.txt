**Q2. The reductio, and what to record.** External prior art says our scaling

consumes two numbers: findings seen by one reviewer, and by two.

With S=2050, Q1=2048, Q2=2, m=5: Chao2 bias-corrected = **561,017**, incidence
jackknife = **3,688**, completeness **0.24–0.37%**. Two estimators from one
family differing by 152×.

- Is that a broken matcher, or is the finding space genuinely near-unbounded?
- **Design the minimum recording change**: the occasion × finding incidence
  matrix, occasion = (model, prompt-role, seed, round). What exactly should be
  recorded, where, and what does it cost per round?
- How would you MEASURE matcher precision and recall here, cheaply?
