## Three rules for this review

1. **"SOUND" IS A FIRST-CLASS VERDICT.** If a thing is right, say it is right,
   say what you checked, and move on. Do not manufacture a finding to justify
   the dispatch. A review that returns "these four are sound, this one is
   broken, here is the fix and here is the measurement showing it works" is
   worth more than one that returns five faults.
2. **EVERY RECOMMENDATION MUST CARRY A FIX-MEASUREMENT.** Not evidence that the
   defect exists — evidence that your proposed remedy changes the outcome. Run
   it. You have a disposable worktree, write access in it, and the extraction
   path works: anything you write there comes back to me and I will read it,
   run it, and test it myself before adopting anything.
3. **JUSTIFY AGAINST THE PROJECT'S AIMS, NOT JUST THE LOCAL BUG.** The
   specification is below. A recommendation that fixes a symptom while cutting
   against the aims is worse than no recommendation.

**Suite baseline:** `python3 -m pytest bench/tests -q` gives **4,839 passed, 0

## The specification you have never been given

**What CDSFL is for.** A researcher has a STEM artefact — a proof, a model, a
module, a paper — and wants to know what is wrong with it before the world
finds out. CDSFL runs a panel of AI models over it across several rounds. A
finding becomes true only when a TOOL re-executes a model-supplied falsifier and
that falsifier fires on the artefact and stays quiet on a corrected copy.
**Agreement between models decides nothing, ever.** The loop stops when a
convergence gate fires on the settled registry.

**The founding constraints, which no recommendation may violate:**
- Tools decide, not votes. Nothing may reintroduce agreement as a truth
  criterion, however convenient.
- Fixes are suggested to a human, never auto-applied.
- Every claim must be falsifiable and every number must be reproducible from
  the artefacts.
- A human is in the loop by design. Minimal-HIL is the target; zero-HIL is not.

**The aim now, in the founder's words:** *"to make the harness useful and
scalable across whatever degree of resources might be useful and available to
any given researcher. Maybe they won't have the luxury of using 5-6 vendor
models, maybe they will be a large institution with access to potentially
unlimited resources, maybe they will be like me with access to only moderate
resources."*
