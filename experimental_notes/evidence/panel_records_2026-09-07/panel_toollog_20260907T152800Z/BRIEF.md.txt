## Change 2 — a new checker

stated interval side by side and reports pairs that disagree arithmetically.

It CANNOT catch the Change 1 defect, and says so: [99.88%, 100.00%] is exactly
right for 3181 of 3181, so that sentence was internally consistent and merely

Questions: run it. Does it produce false positives or false negatives on this
