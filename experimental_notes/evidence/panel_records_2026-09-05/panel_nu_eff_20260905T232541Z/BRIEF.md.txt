## The proposal under adjudication

Replace the re-injection charge `ν` with `ν_eff = ν / |D|`, where `|D|` is the number of
sites a simplification is declared to cover, declared BEFORE the simplification is proposed.

## What is already established BY TOOL. Do not re-derive; attack or extend.

Verified 2026-09-05 with SymPy, z3 and Wolfram, agreeing:

1. Break-even re-injection `ν*` derived from the runner's own phases:
   `ν* = σRq / (1 − qR(1−σ))`, and at `σ=1` it reduces exactly to `qR`.
2. **`ν*`'s free symbols are exactly `{R, q, σ}`.** No `K`, no `w_k`, no scope or reach
   measure of any kind.
3. `∂R_new/∂ν = 1 − R_base`, and **z3 finds no counterexample** to its strict positivity on
   `R_base ∈ [0,1)`. Blast radius is therefore *unconditionally* costly.
4. Phase 3 (`R_k(i) = R_base(1−ν) + ν`) is applied PER CLASS k, so a fix holding at 1000
   sites earns benefit ×K and is charged re-injection ×K. The benefit/cost ratio is K-free.
5. Separately verified: `π` is absent from the update rule and the marginal gain, but
   PRESENT in the answer via `R_k(0) = π`. `PAPER.md:170` states this precisely; the
   appendix Stage table compresses it to "π vanishes", which is lossy.

## cc2's claim, stated as sharply as possible so it can be attacked

Because `ν*` contains no reach term, the model gives a one-line fix that holds at ONE site
and a one-line fix that holds at a THOUSAND sites the identical verdict. So the model does
not merely fail to encode the founder's distinction — **it encodes its negation**, asserting
that simplicity and sufficiency always compete. `ν_eff = ν/|D|` restores reach-sensitivity,
is not piecewise, and collapses to the present model exactly at `|D| = 1`.

## The counter-position, stated as sharply as possible so it can be defended

Compressive simplicity is a DETERMINISTIC IDENTITY claim (S ≡ F over declared scope D).
`ν` is a FORWARD-LOOKING STOCHASTIC RATE. No value of a probability can distinguish "small
because it captured the structure" from "small because something was dropped". Putting
admissibility inside `R_k` makes the equation piecewise on a proof obligation, which is the
opposite of the collapse that is the model's verified core. The right home is a discharge
rule over claims, not a term in the equation. Adding a term to a model that has survived
every attack for 6 months, to encode something the reduction theorems already express, is
the kind of elaboration this project exists to refuse.

## The questions. Answer all 4.

**Q1. Is claim (2)+(3) — no reach term, and blast radius unconditionally costly — a DEFECT
in the model, or a correct modelling choice?** It is not self-evidently a defect: it may be
right that a change touching more code is riskier per unit of benefit. Derive, do not assert.

**Q2. If it IS a defect, is `ν_eff = ν/|D|` the correct repair?** Test it. Does it stay
non-piecewise? Does it reduce to the present model at `|D|=1`? Does it introduce a
perverse incentive — can `|D|` be inflated to buy admissibility? What stops that?

**Q3. Can the founder's distinction be encoded WITHOUT touching the governing equation?**
The reduction criterion as documentation plus a discharge rule is the alternative on the
table. If that suffices, say so and show why the `ν*` reach-blindness then does no harm.

**Q4. What would REFUTE your own answer?** Name the executable check.
