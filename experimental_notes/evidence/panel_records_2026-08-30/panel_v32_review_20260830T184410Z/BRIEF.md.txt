## Root cause found, and the repairs made

**R1 — the simulation seam was one of nine.** The shim patched
`_dispatch_single_model`. Nine functions in the runner dispatch to a model:
`_apply_routing`, `_post_convergence_sweep`, `_inround_reask`,
`_dispatch_single_model`, `_verification_step`, `run_preflight`,
`run_experiment`, `resolve_fn`, `_arb_dispatch`. The other eight went to real,
unconfigured models. The costliest was `resolve_fn` — the routing ladder's
FALSIFIER WRITER — whose call raised, was swallowed by a bare `except` returning
`""`, and logged "routing: 0 resolved by strong writer" in all four rounds.
Repair: the shim now patches the primitive `dispatch_to_model`.
Pinned by `bench/tests/test_sim_seam_covers_every_dispatch_2026-08-30.py`.

**R2 — co-discovery could never fire for the case it exists for.**
`record_codiscovery` (added 2026-08-23 to close a measured defect: 566 findings,
exactly 1.00 `source_aliases` each, zero cross-model co-discovery) resolved with
`lookup_alias(<the DUPLICATE's model>, <the ORIGINAL's finding_id>)`. In a
cross-model duplicate those belong to different models, so the lookup always
missed. Measured on the run: 17 `duplicate_of` records, every one a
foreign-model finding ID, `source_aliases` still exactly 1 on all 19 entries.
Repair: `lookup_alias_any()` resolves by finding ID, refusing ambiguity.
Pinned by `test_codiscovery_resolves_cross_model_2026-08-30.py`.

**R3 — the fix-efficacy probe was gated on a field nothing populates.** It
required `proposed_fix` AND `falsifier_code`; 19 of 19 entries had a fix and 0
had a falsifier, so it reached nothing and left NO TRACE of why. Repair:
`fix_efficacy_decision()` admits on `proposed_fix` alone and records a
`NOT_PROBED_NO_FALSIFIER` outcome, without consuming the per-round cap and
without marking the entry attempted.
Pinned by `test_fix_efficacy_records_why_it_did_not_run_2026-08-30.py`.

**R4 — vendor-keyed logic vs the `-SIM` naming rule.** Two sites compared
`mc.label == "CC2"`; one is the verification stage, which otherwise returns
`{"skipped": True}` silently. `base_model_label()` resolves the suffix.

**R5 — test skips.** 34 skipped tests were audited. 27 removed: 24 in
`test_operational_scripts.py` (the skip dropped an import proof and an
unknown-flag proof), 3 in `test_runner_argv_guard.py` (a blanket skip for
argparse runners that asserted nothing; now an ordering assertion that
`parse_args()` is reached before anything costly). A real defect fell out:
`scripts/note_vagueness_lint.py` exited 0 on a missing path, so a typo'd
filename reported "0 findings" and read as a clean note.
