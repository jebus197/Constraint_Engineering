## What was established, by tool, and is not in dispute

A proposal to replace `ν` with `ν_eff = ν/|D|` was refuted twice over, and both refutations
are Wolfram-verified. It stays refuted. Do not re-argue it. The two decisive kills:

* **Gate escape is closed-form.** `ν/D ≤ ν*` is *exactly* `D ≥ ν/ν*` (z3: `unsat` on any
  divergence). Wolfram `Reduce[ForAll[{ν,t}, … Exists[D, D≥1 ∧ ν/D ≤ t]]]` returns **True**:
  admissibility is always purchasable by declaration.
* **Category error.** `ν` is a probability; `ν/|D|` is not the probability of anything. It
  corrupts the substrate ceiling: with a fix that breaks something *with certainty*,
  declaring `|D|=1000` drops the model floor to **0.3007** where the truth is **1.0**.

Also established: all 5 of the appendix's reductions are **regular** limits (value at the
point equals the limit approaching it; SymPy and Wolfram agree), so this is the well-behaved
case in Batterman's sense — no singular limits, no asymptotic borderland.

## THE FINDING YOU ARE ASKED TO SOLVE

Two seats independently defended the model by locating reach in it. Both were right about
the *model* and wrong about the *runner*.

**Home 1 — `ν`.** `MATHEMATICAL_APPENDIX.md:208` defines it reach-sensitively: *"Localised
one-line changes have low ν. Changes to shared interfaces have higher ν."*
**But `ν` is never computed.** `reference_runner_v3.py:9417` and `:9499` carry
`nu_b: float = 0.05, nu_f: float = 0.20` as literal defaults, and no code writes a
per-finding `ν` from blast radius. Every experiment from 37 to 49 ran on those constants.

**Home 2 — `σ`.** `∂ν*/∂σ = qR(1−qR)/(1+qR(σ−1))²`, strictly positive (Wolfram), so `ν*`
rises with `σ`. At `q=0.21, R=0.3` a fix with `σ=1` gets **937×** the risk budget of one with
`σ=0.001`. **But `σ` is `sk_result.sk`**, and `compute_sk` (`:9237`) takes exactly
`fix_text, source, source_path, baseline, test_cmd, declared_target_kind`. **No input carries
reach.** `S_k` is the product of `g1_ast`, `g2_compile`, `e2_regression`, `e3_ruff`,
`e4_bandit` — it measures whether a fix *parses, compiles, passes tests, lints and scans
clean*. A fix resolving 1 of 1000 sites scores identically to one resolving all 1000.

So the mathematics encodes reach twice and the implementation realises it zero times, and
nothing anywhere reports the gap. `bench/input_complexity.py` exists and appears to have no
caller.

## The questions

**Q1. Which home is correct — `ν`, `σ`, both, or neither?** Derive it. `ν` is
re-injection risk (does the fix BREAK something new); `σ` is fix efficacy (does the fix
RESOLVE the flaw). Reach plausibly belongs to both but with opposite signs. Settle it from
the model's own semantics, not from preference.

**Q2. Propose the concrete implementation.** Runnable. Where does the number come from —
AST diff size, call-graph fan-out, number of SEARCH/REPLACE blocks, files touched, declared
class size? It must be MEASURED from the fix and the target, never declared by a model, or
it inherits the `ν_eff` gate escape. Name the function, its inputs, and its range.

**Q3. What breaks if you wire it?** Every archived run used constants. Turning on a computed
`ν` changes `R_k` and therefore convergence for every future run and breaks comparability
with all 49 completed experiments. Is that acceptable, and how is it managed — shadow mode,
a flag, a recalibration? The queue alarm halts runs at a bound of 2; would a computed `ν`
make halts more or less likely?

**Q4. What is the cheapest honest interim?** If wiring it is too large a change to make
tonight, what is the smallest step that stops the gap being SILENT? This project's repeated
failure is not the defect but that nothing reports it.

**Q5. What would refute your proposal?** Name the executable check.
