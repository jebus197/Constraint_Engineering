## Part 2 — the real question

`compute_rk` clamps non-finite inputs to a conservative end per parameter:
`R_old -> 1.0`, `q -> 0.0`, `sk -> 0.0`, `nu_b -> 1.0`, `nu_f -> 1.0`.

`R_old -> 1.0` was chosen because "unknown risk is maximum risk". But R = 1 is also
the exact point where the break-even polynomial is identically zero, so
`sk_break_even` returns None there.

**Is coercing an unknown R_old to 1.0 correct, or does landing corrupt input on the
one degenerate point make things worse?** Derive it. If you think it is wrong, give
the simplest sufficient alternative. If you think it is right, say why the
degeneracy is harmless.

Use your shell. Be economical — a few decisive checks, not many.
