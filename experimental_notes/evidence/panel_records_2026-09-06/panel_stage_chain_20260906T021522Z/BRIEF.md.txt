## The claim under test

`docs/MATHEMATICAL_APPENDIX.md:165` states, as canon:

> "The mathematical model evolved through five stages. **Each is a strict generalisation of the
> previous** — the earlier model is a special case of the later one under simplifying assumptions."

The 6 stages: (1) `C(n) = 1-(1-p)^n`; (2) `F_n = Σ w_k[1-Π(1-d_i p_ik)]`; (3)
`R_n = Σ w_k (π_k m_k)/((1-π_k)+π_k m_k)` with `m_k = Π(1-q_ik)`; (4)
`R_k(i) = R_k(i-1)(1-q)/(1-q R_k(i-1))`, `R_k(0)=π_k`; (5) three-phase with η, σ, ν;
(6) `η = η_int(1 - c_ext(1-ν_k))`.

## What CC1 measured (reproduce or refute it — `scripts/verify_five_stage_chain.py`)

**4 of the 5 links hold.** 1→2 ✓ (F_n at d=1,K=1,uniform p IS C(n)). 3→4 ✓ and it is an
*identity*: the recursion's closed form `1/(1+((1-π)/π)/(1-q)^n)` equals the batch posterior
exactly, because one step multiplies the ODDS `(1-R)/R` by exactly `1/(1-q)`. 4→5 ✓ (three-phase
at η=1, σ=1, ν=0 IS Stage 4). 5→6 ✓ (at c_ext=0).

**Link 2→3 is not a generalisation.** F_n outputs COVERAGE; R_n outputs RESIDUAL RISK. Solving
`π m/((1-π)+π m) = 1-m` for π gives `π = (1-m)/(1-m+m²)`, which DEPENDS ON m — so no constant
prior makes R_n emit F_n's number, and R_n cannot output the thing it supposedly generalises.
SymPy and Wolfram agree.

**But the two are inter-derivable.** Writing risk in terms of coverage gives a Möbius transform
with determinant `π(π-1)`, non-zero for π ∈ (0,1), inverse `C = (π-R)/(π-πR)`, round-trip
residual exactly 0. At π=1/2 it is simply `R = (1-C)/(2-C)`. So the collapsed equation is C(n) in
risk coordinates — the SAME information, a different coordinate system, not a different fact.

**The collapse itself is CORRECT.** Stage 4 at π=0.5, d=1, q=p equals `(1-p)^n/(1+(1-p)^n)`
exactly (SymPy, mpmath 40 dp, Wolfram).

**Reduction is many-to-one.** F_n, D(n) and G_n are structurally distinct and all reduce to C(n),
so "special → general" is not a derivation: from C(n) you cannot recover which parent produced it.

**Q2.** What should `:165` say instead? It must stay short and true. Candidate: distinguish
