## PART 1b — A CONSEQUENCE NOBODY TRACED, AND IT NEEDS A RULING

Applying the access-only ruling left 3 tests red, and they are ONE defect with a
real question behind it. An ARCHIVED brief,
`bench/logs/panel_round11_2026-09-11/BRIEF.md`, declares
`real-rejection rate : 2/640 = 0.3125%`. Under the narrowed gate the rate is
1/640, so a figure in the HISTORICAL RECORD stopped re-executing — not because
the corpus moved, but because a RULE changed beneath it, retroactively, for
every date.

The project already anticipated the corpus case. `--as-of` pins the DENOMINATOR
to the brief's own date, and `test_declared_figure_as_of_2026-09-29.py` records
the ruling: "the record is NOT edited, and archived briefs are NOT exempted".
That mechanism cannot absorb a rule change, because the numerator now differs at
every date.

This is panel dispute D-5's blast radius, and D-5's own remedy does not cover it:
committing the CRITERION protects the oracle, but an archived DECLARED FIGURE is
a second kind of commitment that the amendment breaks.

- Q5. How should the record handle a figure invalidated by a RULE change rather
  than by corpus drift? Candidates, and you may propose better: make `--as-of`
  pin the RULE SET as well as the corpus, keeping the superseded rules alive so
  historical figures stay reproducible forever; or register the rule change in a
  committed amendments register that the validator consults; or something else.
  Editing the archived brief is NOT on the table. Whatever you propose, deliver
  it as a file and demonstrate it failing when it should.
