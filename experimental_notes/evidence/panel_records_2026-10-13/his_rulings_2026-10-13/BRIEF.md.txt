## ITEM 1 — WHAT IS SEVERITY COMPUTED FROM?

**The record.** `docs/MATHEMATICAL_APPENDIX.md` §7.7 specifies `Sev(f) = W(class) · confidence · V(verification)`, with `W(HARD)=1.0`, `W(SOFT)=0.5`, `V(True)=1.0`, `V(None)=0.5`, `V(False)=0.0`, and the section's stated KEY PROPERTY that a disproved finding scores zero regardless of confidence. **It was never implemented.** `bench/reference_runner_v3.py:6682` reads `severity=float(entry.get("severity") or 0.0)` from the model's own output. **2 of the 3 inputs were never collected:** across 3,298 archived findings `confidence` appears on 0 and no field carries a HARD/SOFT class. The appendix's own key property is violated on **35 of 40** disproved findings — 87.5000%, Wilson 95% [73.8879%, 94.5405%]. Producer: `scripts/severity_was_never_computed_from_the_model_2026-10-12.py`.

**His ruling, verbatim:** *"Severity was supposed to be calculated in accordance with our existing maths model. If the models are asked to compute anything it should surely be computed from our maths model and from what this already says about severity surely, then they must provide a proof to demonstrate that they have computed severity correctly, then the other machinery of the schema should apply a working falsifier/use tools to check that this number makes sense and checks out. This does not qualify as a 'vote'. Indeed it is the very opposite of a vote."*

**MY PROPOSED FIX, for you to attack.** Four parts. (1) Collect the 2 missing inputs: the model supplies `confidence` in [0,1] and classifies its finding HARD or SOFT — both are existing project vocabulary, not new terms. (2) The harness COMPUTES `Sev(f)` and the model never asserts a severity number at all; the field becomes derived, not reported. (3) The proof the model must supply is of ITS OWN arithmetic — W, confidence and their product — which the harness re-derives, exactly as `_validate_rk_computation` already re-derives R_k. (4) The tool check is `V`, which is the falsifier verdict the harness already owns, so a disproved finding scores zero by construction and the gate cannot be voted open.

**Why I think it answers his "opposite of a vote" point:** 2 of the 3 factors become harness-owned, and the one the model supplies is multiplied by a factor the harness can set to zero.

**What I am least sure of, and want you to settle.** The schema change invalidates replay comparability with every archived run. Measured cost of applying only the V factor today: the share at or above the 0.7 cut falls from 41.6313% to 16.3129%, with **835 of 3,298** crossing downward — 25.3184%, Wilson [23.8636%, 26.8306%]. Is that movement evidence the formula is right, or evidence the 0.7 cut was calibrated against the uncomputed float and must move with it? And `confidence` is still a model-asserted number: does multiplying it by harness-owned factors make it not-a-vote, or merely a quieter one?

## ITEM 2 — IS AN EMPTY LADDER THE SAME THING AS NOTHING HAVING READ THE FINDING?

**His framing, verbatim, and he names both horns himself:** *"So is this a tension? If I say the empty ladder counts for nothing, sub-critical findings go unresolved? But if I say they also should be fed back into the harness machinery, this could block or severely delay convergence? The first is not ideal, because it could mean whole classes of faults go unresolved and are ignored. The second could lead to a problem we encountered in the past where new sub-critical (and often mostly churn/dupe) issues kept being discovered and kept pushing convergence beyond reach? Again neither outcome is ideal (if my reading is accurate?)."*

**His reading IS accurate, and the record supports both horns.** The release door that excused never-read findings was withdrawn on his ruling of 2026-10-12. The halt machinery now admits a served-but-unread finding at any severity. The case still unhandled: a sub-critical served to an EMPTY ladder — no writer available besides the finding's own source — acquires `routing_deferred` and is then excluded by the severity skip, so it blocks, is named, and reaches no halt path. Including it was tried on 2026-10-12 and REVERTED, because it broke the equivalence between `_irreducible_queue_split` and the reference rule in `test_the_alarm_names_which_kind_2026-10-02.py`, on a third successive correction to the same predicate inside an hour.

**MY PROPOSED FIX, for you to attack.** Separate the two questions the single predicate is conflating. (1) **Was it read?** A ladder-empty deferral means NOTHING read it — that is a resourcing fact about the roster, not a ruling about the finding — so it must not be excused. (2) **Can anything in this run read it?** If the ladder is empty, nothing can, so blocking further rounds buys nothing: the honest terminal state is HIL escalation with an evidence bundle, which TERMINATES rather than excuses. So: a never-read finding escalates to the founder rather than either blocking for ever or being dropped. (3) **The churn horn is a different defect and needs a different fix:** duplicate and near-duplicate sub-criticals pushing convergence out of reach is a DEDUPLICATION failure, and excusing findings to compensate would hide it. If that is what happened before, the fix belongs in the dedup path, measured on the archive, not in the blocker count.

**What I want from you:** is my (2) sound, or does escalating a sub-critical to HIL on every empty ladder flood the founder and amount to the same thing as excusing it? And measure the churn horn on the archive: how often did sub-critical duplicates actually push a run's novelty series upward? If that is rare, his second fear is smaller than it feels.

## ITEM 3 — THE HALT BOUND IS 2 AND THE ALARM NEEDS MORE THAN 2

**His ruling:** *"Again the goal in all cases is honest clean convergence. Ask the panel how to resolve this too, given this framing."*

**The record.** `cfg.max_irreducible_queue` defaults to 2 and `build_irreducible_queue_alarm` fires only ABOVE it, so 1 or 2 unresolvable never-read findings block convergence without producing the evidence bundle, and the run reaches `max_rounds` instead. Measured on `study_run1b_2026-10-03`: its 2 survivors put the queue at exactly 2.

**MY PROPOSED FIX, for you to attack.** The bound is a single integer applied to classes that differ in whether anything could ever resolve them, and that is the error. A LADDER-EXHAUSTED finding has been tried by every rung, so waiting is reasonable and 2 is defensible. A NEVER-READ finding cannot be resolved by waiting at all, so its bound should be 1: tell the founder at the first occurrence. That makes convergence honest without burning rounds against an impossibility.

**What I want from you:** is a per-class bound right, or is it 2 parameters where 1 would do? And does firing at 1 make the alarm so frequent that it stops being read, which would be a worse failure than the current silence?

**The 4 defects, all raised by panel models in June 2026, recorded UNCONFIRMED and UNTOOLABLE, confirmed 2026-09-10 and verified live on 2026-10-13.** C0001: `_prune_for_coherence` makes the metric it optimises WORSE. **This is the item where I most want you to disagree if you can.** Retiring a mechanism requires a COMMITTED MEASUREMENT showing the replacement dominates on a named property — that is this project's additive standard, and "trim by hand" is not yet such a measurement. So: name the property, and say what measurement would establish that a hand-trimmed directive set dominates the automatic pruner.
