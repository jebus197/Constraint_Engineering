**This is the founder's own instruction, dated 2026-10-02 and unexecuted since.** His words: *"Also implement this and test it on the upcoming simulated runs."* And his ruling of 2026-10-19 on how to settle it: *"Ask the panel what the best approach of all the available options is, given the full specification of the project and what we aim to achieve (clean honest convergence). ## REQUIRED SECTION 3 — Produce a fix, and test it

**A finding without a fix is incomplete, and a fix whose falsifier has not been EXECUTED is a hypothesis.**

**WHERE THE FIX MUST BE DELIVERED: written INTO the sandbox repository tree at its real path, as a file.** A fix described in prose, or left in scratch space, is destroyed at teardown and is not delivered. Write the changed module at the path it actually occupies, and write your test under `bench/tests/` at a real filename. Then RUN the test and report what it printed.

Your fix must satisfy 3 standards explicitly, and say how it does:

1. **ADDITIVE** — nothing removed or narrowed without a committed measurement showing the replacement dominates on a named property. Leaving the channel informative is permitted and needs no such measurement; promoting it does.
2. **SIMPLEST SUFFICIENT** — if a 1-line change and a new subsystem both answer the question, the 1-line change is correct.
3. **COMPOSABILITY** — say whether your answer composes with the commissioning measurement already built, or replaces part of it.

**"STAY INFORMATIVE" IS A VALID FIX** and still requires the same rigour: if that is your answer, deliver the test that would make the project notice the day the evidence changes, and name the threshold at which promotion becomes warranted.

## REQUIRED SECTION 5 — Output shape

Return these fields, in this order, so that 2 replies can be compared without a parser guessing at them.

1. **VERDICT** — one of `PROMOTE_TO_SIGMA`, `PROMOTE_TO_S_K`, `PROMOTE_TO_R_K`, `STAY_INFORMATIVE`. One token, on its own line.
2. **REASONING** — the premises, each with the execution that establishes it. A premise with no executed evidence beside it does not count.
3. **FALSIFIER AND ITS EXECUTED RESULT** — the command you ran and the output it produced, quoted. Not a description of what it would produce.
4. **THE FIX** — every file you wrote, by its real path, with what each one changes and why.
5. **STANDARDS** — how your fix meets ADDITIVE, SIMPLEST SUFFICIENT and COMPOSABILITY, each answered separately.
6. **WHAT WOULD REFUTE THIS** — the measurement and the numeric threshold.
7. **DISAGREEMENT** — with this brief, and with the other seat as you anticipate it.
8. **TERMINATION** — how many passes you ran and what the last one produced.

## REQUIRED SECTION 6 — Termination

**Stop at diminishing returns, which is this project's own criterion:** stop when a further pass produces no new finding above the real-world-consequence threshold, and say in your reply what that last pass produced so the claim can be checked. A finding is above threshold if missing it could cause a real failure, an invalid result or an unsafe condition.

Do not pad passes to reach a number, and do not stop early with a HARD assumption untested. State the count you reached and why you stopped there.
