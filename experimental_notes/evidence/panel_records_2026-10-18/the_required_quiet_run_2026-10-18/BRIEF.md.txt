# PANEL BRIEF — is a longer quiet run the right lever against premature convergence?

## REQUIRED SECTION 1 — The question

**The required quiet run was raised from 3 rounds to 7 to suppress premature convergence. Was that the right lever, or is the other identified lever — more seats — better, and what would each cost?**

The founder ruled on 2026-10-08, verbatim: *"Verdict. Fix it and check your fix with the free panel."* The fix has since been applied. **This brief is the second half of that ruling, which is the check.** It is task C12 on the master task list.

**The artefact under review** is the convergence gate in `bench/reference_runner_v3.py`: the constant `REQUIRED_QUIET_RUN`, the config field `gamma_alt_consecutive_zero_crit` it feeds, and the function `_check_gamma_alt_convergence` that reads it. The measurement behind the change is `bench/the_false_quiet_rate_is_not_the_gates_rate_2026-10-08.py`.

**THE DEFECT.** The gate may declare convergence after a run of consecutive rounds producing zero new critical findings. At a required run of 3, the measured chance that such a run arises WITHOUT the problem space being exhausted is **0.126374** — roughly 1 run in 8 converging prematurely. The panel finding that raised it put the figure at 0.1159 and stated: *"The only levers identified are a longer required quiet run, or more seats."*

**THE FIX APPLIED.** `REQUIRED_QUIET_RUN` is now 7. The committed measurement reports the premature-window rate falling 0.126374 at 3, through 0.0784 at 4, 0.0458 at 5 and 0.0263 at 6, to **0.0000 at 7**. Against a 0.05 target the smallest sufficient value is **5 on the point estimate and 7 on the upper confidence bound**, and the producer's own note is that the upper bound is the honest one to spend a decision on, because the denominator shrinks as the window lengthens. 7 was chosen for that reason.

**THE COST, which is why this needs checking rather than celebrating.** Each extra required quiet round is an extra round of every seat's time on every run that would otherwise have converged. The default round ceiling is 21, so a 7-round quiet requirement consumes a third of the budget in confirmation alone. Against that, all 3 shakedown legs converged at rounds 3, 4 and 4 — meaning a 7-round quiet requirement would have prevented every one of them from converging when it did.

**AND A SECOND FINDING THAT BEARS ON THE CHOICE.** The same producer reports that the gamma arm of the two-sided gate **blocked zero premature windows**, so the joint false-quiet rate equals the quiet-window arm's rate alone. The 2 arms are positively dependent: median gamma is 0.497 inside a quiet run against 0.3939 elsewhere, Mann-Whitney one-sided p = 0.0022. So the gate's second arm is not providing the independent protection its name implies, and lengthening the first arm may be compensating for an arm that does not work.
