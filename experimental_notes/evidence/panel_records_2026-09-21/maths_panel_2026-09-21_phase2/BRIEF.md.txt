## Section 2 — THE QUESTION. A modelling choice, not an arithmetic error.

The appendix's Phase 2 reads:

> **Phase 2 — Resolution.** The fix may or may not resolve the target flaw: `R_base = σ · R_det + (1 − σ) · R_old`. When σ = 1, the full detection benefit is captured. **When σ = 0, risk stays at R_old — the fix failed and risk reverts to the pre-detection level.**

Phase 1 sets `R_det = R_old(1−q)/(1−q·R_old)`, which appendix section 1 derives as `P(flaw | no detection)` — a Bayesian POSTERIOR.

**CC1's position, for you to attack.** At σ = 0 the appendix returns risk to `R_old`, discarding the posterior. But the evidence still happened. A fix failing does not un-observe the pass that produced `R_det`. If that reading is right, Phase 2 conflates "the repair did not work" with "the observation did not occur", and only the first is what σ measures.

The proposed revision 1.1 acts on the posterior instead: `R_new = (1−s)·z + b·(1−z)` with `z` the posterior and `s = σ(1−ν)`, `b = ν`. At σ = 0 it holds `z`.

**Executed, and cross-verified on 4 tools.** SymPy gives the difference between the appendix composite and the mapped revision as `(ν−1)(−R_old·σ + R_old + 2σz − z)`, zero only on the STATE condition `R_old = z(2σ−1)/(σ−1)` — a condition on where you are, not a restriction on parameters, so the two are not the same map. NumPy over 200,000 random points in the open unit cube: 200,000 of 200,000 differ by more than 1e-12, maximum gap 0.959481, mean 0.124414, and both forms stay inside [0,1]. mpmath at 40 digits, at `R_old=0.5, q=0.3, σ=0.4, ν=0.1`: appendix 0.5182352941176470588, revision 0.3223529411764705882, gap 0.1958823529411764706. z3 finds the two agree at 2 distinct states simultaneously only at σ=0, ν=1.

**A PRIOR CLAIM IN THIS PROJECT'S OWN RECORD IS NARROWED BY THAT, AND CC1 MADE IT.** The 2026-09-20 synthesis says the revision's action step "is identical to the appendix Phase-3 form under `s = σ(1−ν)`, `b = ν`, symbolic difference exactly 0". Re-executed, that is true of **Phase 3 ALONE** — the bare re-injection map `x(1−ν)+ν` — which both forms share at σ=0. It is NOT true of the appendix's Phase 2 and 3 COMPOSITE. The identity is real and much narrower than the sentence reads.
