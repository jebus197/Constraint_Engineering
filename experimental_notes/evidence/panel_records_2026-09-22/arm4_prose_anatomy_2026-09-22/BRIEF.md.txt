# Panel brief — what UNTOOLABLE means on a prose target

## SECTION 1 — The question

Does `UNTOOLABLE`, as arm 4 assigned it to 6 of 17 findings, mean "there was nothing here to
compute", or does it mean "the machinery gave up while it still had rungs and still held
source it had produced"? The artefacts under review are
`scripts/arm4_untoolable_anatomy_2026-09-22.py` (CC1's measurement, written this morning and
checked by nobody), the run it reads at
`bench/logs/commissioning_arm4_prose_20260922T053349Z/commissioning_arm4_prose_report.json`,
and the code that assigns the verdict in `bench/reference_runner_v3.py`.

Your task is to find what is WRONG with CC1's reading, not to confirm what is right.

Background. That run is the only archived one putting the panel machinery against a WRITTEN
SPECIFICATION (`bench/BUILD_BOT_TEST_BENCH_FIX_SPEC.md`) rather than a program: 5 simulated
seats, 17 findings, 1 round. The founder's design position, stated 2026-09-10 and restated
2026-09-22: a target that is PURELY prose should be recorded as having nothing to compute,
while any computationally accessible element INSIDE a prose target should still be solved.
His concern is that STEM material which happens to contain prose gets written off.

CC1 reports, from that registry: 7 of 17 findings reached CONFIRMED, so the prose path is not
inert; all 6 UNTOOLABLE entries carry falsifier source in `routing_history.last_falsifier_code`

## SECTION 2 — Use the harness

Form your answer by RUNNING the machinery, not by describing it.

`S_k` is the instrument that decides this question, because the choice between UNTOOLABLE and
REJECTED is a claim about what enters the score. An unscoreable fix is not a defective one, so
if UNTOOLABLE feeds S_k the same way a refutation does, the label is doing damage; if it
withholds from S_k entirely, it is doing nothing and 6 findings left the round unscored. Read
which it is and report the line.

`gamma` is the second instrument, and it bears on the ladder directly. Stopping at 2 rungs of 5
is defensible if and only if it is diminishing returns rather than a cap — that is precisely
what gamma measures. Determine whether the ladder's stop is gamma-governed or constant-governed,
and say which by reading the code that decides it.

The two-sided gate and the severity model are NOT expected to bear on this; say so if you find
otherwise.
