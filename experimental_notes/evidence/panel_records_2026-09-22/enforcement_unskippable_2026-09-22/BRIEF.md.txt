# Panel brief — make FFAFP, sy and Wolfram unskippable for CC1

## SECTION 1 — The question

The founder's instruction, verbatim: *"All I want is for you never to skip the full 'f' (5 step
protocol), or to skip sy, on any element of your output that might be accessible to this. CDSFL
as a whole demands tool use (in this case including Wolfram). We demand this of all our other
models. You should be no exception."*

Asked to choose between a hook that BLOCKS and one that REPORTS, he answered "Block rather than
report", then said plainly that he does not know what block means and that the outcome he wants
is simply that the protocol is never skipped. So the question is NOT "block or report". It is:

**What is the simplest sufficient mechanism that actually prevents CC1 skipping FFAFP, sy and
Wolfram — given that the mechanism built for exactly this purpose already exists, is wired, is
covered by 71 passing tests, and has been silently dead for about 2 days?**

The artefacts under review:
- `~/.claude/hooks/ffafp_audit.py` — the FFAFP trace detector, 809 lines, wired in
  `~/.claude/settings.json` as a UserPromptSubmit hook. NOT in this repository; read it at the
  absolute path, and also at `bench/logs/maths_panel_2026-09-20_r4/sandbox_harvest/cc2/attempt-1/files/hooks/ffafp_audit.py`.
- `~/.claude/.ffafp_audit/a07b3790-0a2a-4978-aedb-bd842c0493d3` — its own state file. Its
  `history` list ENDS at the afternoon of 2026-09-20 while its `offset` advanced today. It is
  consuming the transcript and recording nothing.
- `bench/wolfram_standard.py` and `bench/tools/wolfram_gate/serial` — the Wolfram serial gate.
- `~/.claude/hooks/mc_commands.py` — the existing ex-ante reminder.
- `hooks/work_not_narrate.py` in this repository — the ONLY hook here that can refuse a stop.

## SECTION 1b — What is already established, so you do not re-derive it

An independent analyst established, and CC1 verified against the hook's own state file:

- The FFAFP detector's `history` has 40 entries, the last at the afternoon of 2026-09-20. It has
  appended nothing since while still advancing its byte offset.
- The detector is DELIBERATELY report-only. Its docstring: *"A hook that blocked on a heuristic
  this soft would be switched off within a day, and a false block on correct work costs more
  than a missed reminder."* Exit status is always 0 and no `decision` field is emitted.
- Its own measurement, from its `--survey` mode, is that reminding did not work: `mc_commands.py`
  has injected the sy obligation into EVERY prompt since 2026-08-30, and a STEM tool still
  appeared in under a third of work turns over the corpus it measured.
- On today's transcript its survey reports P-PASS traces MISSING on a substantial minority of
  code-touching turns — CC1 changed a code file and ran nothing that could fail. Run --survey
  yourself and quote your own figure with its interval; do not quote one from this brief,
  because no committed script in this repository produces it.
- On Wolfram: every mechanism built for the founder's 2026-09-17 ruling was wired to SEATS
  through the dispatcher's SYSTEM prompt. NONE of it is wired to CC1. The seats used Wolfram in
  6 of 6 dispatches; CC1 made 1 Wolfram call all day and it landed only after being challenged.

## SECTION 2 — Use the harness

Form your answer by RUNNING things, not by describing them.

`sigma` is the instrument that bears here, because this is a question about whether an
instrument's own reported state can be trusted. A detector that returns "nothing missing" when
it is broken is indistinguishable, downstream, from one that returns "nothing missing" because
nothing was missing — the same false-negative shape the project recorded when 16 of 17 panel
tool calls errored and were read as results. Establish which of those two the silence is, by
execution, and say what a self-check would have to assert to tell them apart.

`S_k` does NOT bear on this and the two-sided gate does not either; say so if you find otherwise.

Run `--survey` yourself against the live transcript before quoting any rate from it.

## SECTION 3 — Produce a fix, and test it

A finding without a fix is half an answer. A fix without a falsifier you have EXECUTED is a
hypothesis.

Deliver every fix by WRITING IT INTO THE SANDBOX REPOSITORY TREE AT ITS REAL PATH. A fix
described in prose is not delivered. A fix written to scratch space is destroyed at teardown.

## SECTION 4 — What would refute you

State what evidence would overturn your own answer before you conclude. If you propose blocking,
name the false-positive case that would make CC1 or the founder switch it off, and say what your
design does about that case. If you propose reporting, name the evidence that reporting works,
given the measurement above that reminding did not.

- `strongest_disagreement` — with this brief's framing, including with the founder's "block"

## SECTION 6 — Termination

Stop on diminishing returns: when a further pass adds no finding above the real-world-consequence
threshold. Report how many passes you ran and what the last one added. Do not manufacture
findings. Do not nitpick style.

Disagreement with the other seat is information. Do not smooth it away.
