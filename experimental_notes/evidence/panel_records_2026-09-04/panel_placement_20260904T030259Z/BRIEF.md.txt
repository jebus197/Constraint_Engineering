## SECTION 1 — THE TWO MATTERS, WITH THE MEASUREMENTS

### Matter A — the rubric and its numeric proxy disagree, and nothing reports it

`CRITICAL_SEVERITY_THRESHOLD = 0.7` is not a free parameter. The work queue records it as
the **operational proxy** for a consequence-based rubric pre-registered and frozen at
`bench/exp40_baseline/CRITICAL_DEFINITION_PREREG_2026-05-18.md`, **which is authoritative
where rubric and number disagree.** Moving 0.7 requires a new dated pre-registration.

Measured 2026-09-02 (runway 0C.8): across 286 archived findings in the [0.65, 0.75) band,
the numeric proxy and the 5-clause rubric agree on **141 of 259 judgeable cases — 54.4%,
Wilson [48.4%, 60.4%]**. Not reader noise: 2 independent blind readers agreed with each
other on 86 of 93 (92.5%, Cohen's kappa 0.837 [0.721, 0.953]) while each agreed with the
number about 55% of the time. Of findings exactly on the threshold, the rubric calls
**67.0%** critical, [57.7%, 75.1%].

The pre-registration requires each run to report (i) the count of findings where rubric and
numeric disagree and (ii) the verdict's sensitivity to that disagreement. **(ii) is built**
(`boundary_band_sensitivity`). **(i) has never been built** — the runner says so itself:
"needs a per-finding rubric classifier and is not wired here." The specified remedy — where
they disagree on a verdict-relevant finding, the rubric governs and the adjudication is
logged — does not exist. The pre-registration also states a machine **cannot** adjudicate
those 5 clauses: a model or the human must.

So on the findings where the gate is actually decided, the run is governed by the number
while a frozen document says the rubric governs, and nothing reports the gap.

### Matter B — where the reduction work belongs

Proposed: a simplification is admissible iff the simpler form S and the fuller form F agree
across a scope D **declared before either was proposed** (residual Δ = ∅), or Δ is named,
bounded, and filed as its own claim with its own test. Established by **proof** or
**exhaustive enumeration**; a confidence interval applies only where D was sampled.
Consequence: **sampling can refute a simplification but can never admit one.**

Three verified data points, all reproducible:
1. The appendix §1.1 already contains a section titled **"Reduction Property"**: under
   K=1, d=1, all p_ik=p, π=0.5, the unified equation collapses to
   `R_n = (1-p)^n / (1 + (1-p)^n)`. Verified: unrolling the recursive form matches the
   closed form with residual exactly 0 for n=1..8, **and the induction step is exactly 0**,
   so it holds for all n by proof. mpmath 30dp agrees to 1e-32.
2. The appendix contains **28** such statements (author previously said 29 — wrong).
   `bench/tests/test_channel_boundary.py:148-176` already grid-asserts one to 1e-9, so
   "zero are tested" was also wrong.
3. The absorb-guard case has a **non-empty** residual and fails the criterion.

**The founder's question, verbatim in substance:** the mathematical appendix is already
vast. If this is recorded there and nothing else is done, it may be buried alongside
mathematics that skilled mathematicians struggle to follow, and become decoration. But if
no machinery is built to test or deploy it, what is the point of it at all? Should it be a
separate prominent entry, or should it form part of the unified/collapsed equation itself?
**And if it did, would the equation keep the very simplicity this work is about, or be
pushed beyond comprehension and credibility?**

Context the founder supplied: the mathematical model has survived every assault so far and
has not been disproven; the founder's position is neutral and explicitly "go with what

Relevant measured constraint: inter-architecture correlation **rho = 0.564 across 289
