    Build the pre-registered threshold profile and boundary census (F6), never implemented
    
    CRITICAL_DEFINITION_PREREG_2026-05-18.md requires, verbatim, that gamma be
    "additionally reported at 0.5 / 0.6 / 0.7 / 0.8 so a reviewer sees the full
    threshold profile, not a single chosen point", and per run "the count of findings
    where rubric and numeric disagreed, and the verdict's sensitivity to that
    disagreement". Measured 2026-09-01: neither exists in the code, and 0 of 50
    archived real reports carries a profile. The frozen definition existed; the
    machinery that makes it auditable did not.
    
    WHY IT MATTERS, over 6,865 real archived findings. There is NO natural break at
    0.70. scipy KDE minima fall at 0.12, 0.37 and 0.62; a numpy histogram puts
    valleys at 0.67 and 0.71, either side of it. 401 findings, 5.8 percent, sit
    EXACTLY on 0.70 because models quantise to round numbers, so the boundary runs
    straight through a spike, which is the most sensitive place a threshold can sit.
    Moving it to 0.72 would reclassify 456 findings, 6.6 percent of the archive.
    
    THE THRESHOLD IS NOT MOVED. The pre-registration names moving it after seeing
    results as the single largest cooking vector, and the founder ruling stands: keep
    0.7, make the pre-registration visible. This is additive only.
    
    _settled_novelty_series takes an optional severity_threshold defaulting to the
    pre-registered proxy, so every existing caller is byte-identical.
    gamma_threshold_profile re-reads the SAME settled registry at each threshold and
    reports gamma, the critical series, both gate sides, and whether the verdict
    depended on the chosen point. boundary_band_census reports the population a
    rubric adjudication must cover, LABELLED IN ITS OWN OUTPUT as a proxy, because
    the five-clause consequence rubric cannot be evaluated mechanically.
    
    Validated against archived exp45: at 0.7 the instrument reproduces the recorded
    12 criticals exactly. Across the profile, gamma runs 0.5012, 0.5394, 0.7468,
    0.7789 with 25, 18, 12 and 5 criticals, and the gate fires at every threshold, so
    verdict_is_threshold_sensitive is False. That STRENGTHENS the exp45 result: its
    convergence did not depend on the chosen point.
    
    THREE BUGS I INTRODUCED AND CAUGHT BY TESTING RATHER THAN READING. The first
    substitution landed inside unverified_critical_count, leaving an undefined _thr
    and a NameError waiting to fire. The second landed in a severity-calibration
    function. The third targeted a literal that appears twice in the target function,
    once in its own docstring. Each was found by asserting the profile actually
    VARIES with the threshold, which it did not until the fix was correct.
