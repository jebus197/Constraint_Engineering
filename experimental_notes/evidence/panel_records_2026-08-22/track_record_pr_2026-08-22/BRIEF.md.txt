# Panel review: is this project's track record sound, or is the work to date unreliable?

You are one of five panellists. There is no synthesis step that forces agreement.
Disagreement is kept as information. Give an independent verdict.

## The question, stated neutrally

CDSFL is a research framework that uses structured Popperian falsification and a
multi-model panel to find defects in STEM artefacts. Its founding principle is
TOOLS DECIDE, NOT VOTES: a finding is confirmed when the runner independently
re-executes a model-supplied falsifier and observes the designed failure, never by
model agreement.

Over the last five days an assistant (CC1) audited the harness and found a series
of defects. Eleven of CC1's own claims were withdrawn in that window after being
checked. The founder now asks whether the cumulative record means the work to date
is unreliable.

Two hypotheses are on the table. They are stated here with equal weight and you
are not being asked to prefer either.

**H-BUILD.** The defects are the ordinary residue of a system that was still being
built. A component cannot be faulted for running imperfectly before it is
finished. Under this hypothesis the finding of defects is evidence the build is
progressing, not evidence the results are void.

**H-VOID.** The defects are not build residue. Conclusions were drawn from
components that were broken at the time, and presented with confidence
disproportionate to their state. Under this hypothesis some or all of the recorded
results should be treated as unreliable.

A third possibility, which you should consider on its merits: **both are partly
true, with the split falling somewhere specific that the data can locate.**

### M6. When the tool verdict and the model majority DISAGREE, which prevails?

227 modern entries carry both a tool verdict and model votes. In 201 the two agree.
In 26 they disagree. Outcome of those 26:

- **the tool verdict prevails: 25**
- **the model majority prevails: 0**
- neither: 1 (exp46 C0014 — tool ERRORED, one model voted CONFIRM, status became
  REFUTED, i.e. more conservative than either party wanted)

Sign test, entry level: 25/25, p = 2.98e-08. The 26 disagreements are spread over
8 distinct runs; in 7 of the 8 the tool prevailed in every disagreement, run-level
sign test p = 0.0078.

Assumption-free restatement, requiring no mapping of what each side "wanted":
**of 16 findings where the model majority voted CONFIRM and the falsifier ERRORED
or was UNTOOLABLE, the number that reached CONFIRMED or CLOSED is ZERO** (binomial
p = 3.05e-05 against a vote-decided null).

### M9. A control run tonight

A null-perturbation control was built and run on the archive: change something in
the target that the finding does not accuse, and a sound falsifier must NOT change

perturbation** (the rename was possible for 304 of them). The complementary
control — repair the claim, the falsifier must go quiet — has never been run.
