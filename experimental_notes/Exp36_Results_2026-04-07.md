## Key Findings

### 1. Burst Reasoning Phenomenon

R8 produced 21 novel findings (72.4% novelty rate) after all 5 models were restarted fresh by ITC. This is the highest single-round novelty since the blind R0 and demonstrates that ITC restart_fresh creates genuine burst reasoning by breaking attentional fixation. The mechanism is analogous to neuronal refractory periods: temporary context clearing restores the model's sensitivity to unexplored regions of the search space.

Full analysis in [Exp36_Burst_Reasoning_Analysis_2026-04-07.md](Exp36_Burst_Reasoning_Analysis_2026-04-07.md).

### 2. Three-Phase Novelty Decay

The novelty trajectory shows three distinct regimes:

**Phase 1 -- Initial exponential decay (R0-R4):** 30, 10, 5, 4, 1. Models rapidly exhaust the most obvious findings. Novelty drops by roughly 50% per round.

**Phase 2 -- Burst injection plateau (R5-R8):** 6, 7, 2, 21. ITC interventions (change_focus then restart_fresh) create a secondary discovery phase. The R8 burst of 21 novel findings demonstrates that the search space was far from exhausted; the models were fixated, not finished.

**Phase 3 -- Slow post-burst decay (R9-R22):** Oscillating 1-9, mean approximately 4.5, with a declining envelope. The system enters a regime where each round produces modest novelty but never drops consistently below the convergence threshold of 2 or fewer novel findings for 2 consecutive rounds.

### 3. ITC-Convergence Feedback Loop

The ITC restart_fresh mechanism and convergence gate are in structural tension. ITC restarts models to maintain discovery quality. Fresh models produce novel findings. Novelty stays above the convergence threshold. The convergence gate fails to close. ITC keeps cycling. This is a genuine design conflict: the system that maintains quality actively prevents the system that detects completion from firing.

ChatGPT accumulated 11 or more consecutive ITC interventions during the run, illustrating the persistence of this loop.

### 4. Contested Findings as Convergence Blocker

The convergence gate requires contested==0. From R12 onwards, 1-2 contested findings persisted through 11 rounds without resolution. Models could not resolve the disagreement autonomously. The gate nearly fired at R18 (novel=2, only contested=1 blocking) but the single persistent disagreement prevented termination.

This is a design flaw: contested findings that models cannot resolve should be escalated to HIL and removed from the convergence gate condition after a configurable number of rounds.

### 5. Churn Signal

The novelty rate (novel/raw) declined from approximately 48% in R0-R4 to approximately 20% in R15-R22. Models produced increasing raw volume with decreasing information gain. Gamma captures the novel depletion trend but not the raw-to-novel ratio divergence. A churn metric (rho = novel/raw, or its inverse) would provide a complementary convergence signal that detects when models are generating high-volume, low-information output.

### 6. Shadow Pipeline Findings

Several v2 pipeline components ran in shadow mode during the experiment:

| Component | Shadow Observation |
|-----------|--------------------|
| DC v2 regex classifier | 21-44% agreement with LLM classifier; systematically misclassifies as "mathematical" |
| B Cell v2 | Produced Z3 SMT counterexample at R14 (formal verification working) |

| RT v2 | More tolerant than v1 (33% removal rate vs v1 flagging rate) |

If active rather than shadowed, the v2 pipeline would reduce canonical count by an estimated 15-20% and likely accelerate convergence by removing redundant findings earlier.

### 7. CC2v Maturation

CC2v (the CC2 verification agent) shifted from high rejection rates in early batches (4/6 rejected) to high confirmation rates by mid-experiment (R16: 5/6 confirmed). The verification agent's accuracy improved as it accumulated more codebase context across rounds. This suggests CC2v benefits from the same cumulative context that causes fixation in discovery models, but in a verification role the effect is positive rather than negative.

### 8. Discovery Density

153 canonical findings from 420 lines of code yields a density of 1 finding per 2.7 lines. This is extremely dense and likely includes significant redundancy even after automated dedup. Post-experiment manual dedup is essential before treating the canonical count as a true measure of unique issues in the file.

### 9. Budget Extension Mechanism

The budget correctly extended from R20 to R24 due to contested findings remaining unresolved. However, the extension was unproductive: contested went from 1 to 2 and novelty rebounded to 6 in R21. The extension mechanism kept the experiment running without resolving the conditions that triggered it. A time-limit on contested resolution within the extension phase would prevent open-ended extensions.

### 10. OPEN Finding Bathtub Curve

OPEN count trajectory across rounds:

```
30, 40, 17, 15, 12, 14, 18, 16, 34, 38, 33, 36, 38, 37, 42, 42, 39, 36, 37, 38, 44, 48, 44
```

This forms a bathtub curve: dropping sharply as initial findings are resolved (R0-R4), then climbing back as burst reasoning injects new findings faster than CC2v verdicts can resolve them (R8 onwards). The rising OPEN count in the second half contributed to the convergence gate remaining unsatisfied.

## Design Improvements Identified

Seven design changes are indicated by this experiment's results:

1. **Contested-to-HIL escalation.** After N rounds of persistent contested status (suggested: 5 rounds), escalate to HIL and remove the finding from the convergence gate condition. The current design allows a single intractable disagreement to block termination indefinitely.

2. **Churn metric.** Add discovery efficiency rho = novel/raw as a secondary convergence signal. When rho drops below a threshold (suggested: 0.15) for 3 consecutive rounds, the system is generating noise. Gamma alone does not capture this.

3. **Consolidation phase.** In the final rounds (suggested: last 3 of budget), ITC should transition from restart_fresh to change_focus only. This allows novelty to decay naturally toward the convergence threshold instead of being artificially sustained by fresh-context bursts.

4. **Decay-rate convergence.** Use rolling average novelty decline rate as a softer convergence criterion alongside the absolute threshold. A system producing consistently fewer novel findings per round is converging even if it occasionally spikes above the absolute threshold.

5. **Regex classifier recalibration.** The DC v1 regex patterns produce 21-44% agreement with the LLM classifier. Either recalibrate the regex patterns or promote the LLM classifier to primary with the regex as fallback.

6. **LLM classifier timeout.** Increase from 15 seconds for long finding descriptions. Timeout-induced classification failures add noise to the pipeline.

7. **v2 shadow activation.** Activate Helper T v2 and B Cell v2 for the next experiment to test whether their dedup and formal verification capabilities improve convergence speed and reduce the OPEN backlog.

## Methodology Notes

This experiment is the longest CDSFL run to date (23 rounds, 224 minutes). The extended runtime exposed structural tensions in the ITC-convergence interaction that shorter experiments could not reveal. The three-phase novelty pattern, the contested-findings blocker, and the ITC feedback loop are all phenomena that require 15 or more rounds to manifest clearly.

The 51 HIL flags (ITC consecutive intervention warnings) indicate the system was working hard to maintain model productivity throughout. This level of ITC activity is significantly higher than prior experiments and is a direct consequence of the longer run combined with the feedback loop described in Finding 3.
