# The Remaining Experimental Arc — Forensic Provenance and a Concrete Roadmap

**2026-07-22, 01:03 BST.**

## Summary

A forensic audit of the planning record (both April plans, both panel-review round outcomes, the Section 8 decision register, the operational tracker, and full git history) establishes four findings. **First**, the founder's recollection is correct: the per-experiment content of the arc tail (old Exp 44–54) was never founder-ratified. It was drafted by CC1, inherited its 14-way structure from Exp 39's sub-experiment configuration, and was closed on 21 April 2026 by a five-model panel under *compelled convergence* — a discussion discipline the founder later retired as "a star-topology HACK." The plan's own design deferred every substantive tail decision to per-experiment founder gates that mostly never fired. **Second**, a new structural finding: the **B-Cell domain-specialist dispatch that the tail experiments exist to validate is not wired into the live runner** (`reference_runner_v2.py` instantiates no specialist dispatch and never reads the `domains/immune/*.toml` configs). The tail's stated pass criteria are unexecutable as written. **Third**, the remaining experiments do not get "dramatically smaller" in target size (10.7K → 23K → 29K → 39K chars, plus four ~15–25K modules that do not yet exist); what shrinks is marginal effort per experiment. **Fourth**, given findings one and two, a principled compression of the remaining arc from 9 experiments to 6 is available and is recommended below, cutting estimated remaining cost to ~$150–240 [estimate].

## 1. How the arc came to be — the provenance chain

1. **The 14-way structure predates the arc.** "Experiment 39 is **not a single experiment**. It is a set of 14 sub-experiments defined in bench/exp39_config.json" (`Exp40_Readiness_and_Novelty_Review_2026-04-17.md:65`). The 17 April execution plan maps these one-to-one onto Exp 40–53 (`Exp40_to_54_Execution_Plan_2026-04-17.md:256–271`). Nobody "designed" a 15-experiment programme from first principles; an existing sub-experiment list was promoted into an arc.
2. **The 17 April plan left the tail open.** Targets for old 47/49/51/52/53: "to determine"; target selection "Status: PARTIAL … others require target-selection session" (`Execution_Plan:280`); Exp 41–53 items "Status: PENDING" (`:326`). The founder's 17 April contribution was directive-level: "sequential build, learn from Exp 40 first" and the runner-freeze rule (`Exp40_Implementation_Progress_2026-04-17.md:77,173`).
3. **The 21 April consolidated plan closed the tail by model panel, not founder.** Tail-target commitments are recorded as panel votes: "committed 21 April (RQ6 5/5 convergence)" (`Exp40_to_54_Consolidated_Plan_2026-04-21.md:45,49–51`). Round 2 "was configured under compelled convergence: every model … had to yield to one existing position or refute a specific position — no new alternatives admitted" (`Exp40_PreLaunch_Code_Changes_Round2_Close_2026-04-21.md:15`). Compelled convergence was later judged "a late desperation patch … consensus-forcing was reached for in desperation when disagreement became painful" (`Divergence_Study_Complete_2026-06-03.md:9`) and retired by founder ruling on 8 July 2026.
4. **Founder sign-off was per-experiment by design, and deferred.** "Gate D — founder sign-off: post-mortem reviewed; any scope change approved" (`Consolidated_Plan:197`); the Section 8 register is titled "Five open founder decisions for the Exp 40–54 arc," each "due at a concrete later trigger" — Exp 54 Cell A at Exp 54 entry, target-article construction "at the entry to each of Experiments 47, 51, 52, and 53," threshold freeze between 53 and 54. The "hand-waving" the founder experienced was therefore structural: the plan literally said *cross each bridge at its entry*.
5. **Git contains no founder ratification of the tail.** All 39 commits in the 15–24 April window are agent-authored sv commits; the record's own conclusion: launch of Exp 40 itself was still "the founder's go or no-go" (`Exp40_to_54_Plan_Section8_Decision_Register_2026-04-21.md:31`). In June the founder explicitly gated further runs: "review what Exp 40–54 actually entail (founder wants to examine the experiment contents before further runs)" (`CDSFL_Agent_Operational_Plan.md:31`). The first genuine founder ruling on the tail's *content* is the 20 July restructure (drop old 44 + 49, renumber, `resources/RECOVERY.md:30`).

**Verdict:** the tail was concretely *drafted* but never concretely *agreed*. The founder ratified the arc's existence, the count-and-numbering framing (20–21 April corrections), each next-experiment hop, and the 20 July restructure — nothing more. Every per-experiment target, specialist assignment, and pass criterion in old 44–54 traces to CC1 drafting plus panel votes under a since-repudiated convergence discipline.

## 2. The un-wired specialist layer (new structural finding)

The April plan's tail is organised around **domain B-Cell specialists**: statistics (old 45), CS/software (old 46), biology (old 47), information science (old 48), physics/chemistry/engineering shadow-flips (old 51/52/53). Verified today: `bench/cdsfl_registry/domains/immune/*.toml` exist, but **no specialist dispatch is instantiated anywhere in live code** — `grep` across `bench/` finds zero non-test instantiation, and `reference_runner_v2.py` (the active runner) contains two passing comment mentions of "specialist" and never reads the domain TOMLs. The 12 April "Phase 6: Specialist B-Cell dispatch wiring (shadow mode)" commit wired the older orchestrator, not runner v2.

Consequences:
- Pass criteria of the form "Statistics specialist verdict count > 0" (old 45), "Biology specialist verdict count > 0" (old 47), and the K/L/M live-flips at old 51/52/53 are **unexecutable on the current runner**.
- What the arc experiments *actually* test — demonstrated by Exp 40–43 — is the five-model panel + runnable-falsifier gate + capability routing + location-keyed two-sided convergence gate on each target. That machinery is real, proven, and generalised (Exp 43).
- The four-way split of the synthesised STEM modules (one per domain) existed to isolate per-domain specialists. With no specialists in the loop, that isolation rationale is void. What survives is the modules' *other* value: ground-truthed STEM content with **planted false claims** ("at least one intentionally false claim … a module that produces only confirmations tells the panel nothing about discriminative capacity," `Consolidated_Plan:66`) — the only direct recall measurement anywhere in the programme, and it works through the ordinary panel+falsifier path.

This finding also corrects the 22 July session's own framing: the previous recommendation of `evidence.py` "exercising the info-science specialist" inherited the unwired assumption. The size-matched shake-out argument for `evidence.py` stands; the specialist argument falls.

## 4. The "getting dramatically smaller" question, corrected

Target sizes do not shrink: 10.7K → 23.2K → 29.1K → 39.0K → 4 × ~15–25K → factorial. Two things do shrink: (a) **marginal effort** — the machinery is proven, configs are templated, faults-per-experiment has fallen (Exp 42 needed a root-cause hunt; Exp 43 needed one bounded gate fix); (b) **experiment count**, if the compression below is adopted (9 remaining → 6). Cost per experiment (~$12–40) is roughly flat, dominated by rounds-to-convergence, not target size.

## 5. Recommended concrete roadmap (single recommendation, founder decides)

0. **Implement FIX 1–5** with the full convergence regression suite (session work, no API cost). Already approved in principle.
1. **NEW 44 = `evidence.py`** — the discriminating shake-out (size-matched to the proven failure regime). ~$15–25.
2. **NEW 45 = `dm/_memory.py`** — second post-fix run; doubles as the panel's scrutiny of the module the founder queried. ~$12–18.
3. **NEW 46 = `dm/_shadow_stage6.py`** — the calibrator audit (executable pass criterion; shadow-live code). ~$20–30.
4. **NEW 47 = `dm/_divergence.py`** — the largest, fully-live module, run once the machinery is proven at scale below it. ~$25–40.
5. **NEW 48 = ONE consolidated synthesised STEM module** (~20–25K), carrying the strongest claim clusters from all four domain briefs including every planted-false-claim class — drafted ahead in-session, reviewed by a cheap panel pass, then run. Replaces four separate modules; the four-way split served specialist isolation that cannot occur. Preserves the ground-truthed recall measurement and the STEM-content bridge to BR2. ~$15–25 (+ ~$3–5 draft review).
6. **NEW 49 = the 2×2 factorial**, target = a second fresh synthesised module (uncontaminated, ground-truth-seeded, size-controlled) rather than runner-tests-runner. Choosing a fresh target resolves the April Cell A 3:2 split as a side effect: the Exp 36–38 archive is for different targets, so Cell A must be a fresh §17-off/§18-off run — the position the residual split already leaned toward. Threshold freeze before entry per the April register. ~$45–80.

Arc total: **~$150–240 [estimate]**, against a balance of $64.51 — roughly $100–180 of further top-up, versus the prior $300–450 projection. The specialist layer and K/L/M flips are explicitly retired from this arc (recorded as such, not silently dropped); if Bench Run 2 proceeds, wiring specialists is a BR2-prep decision taken then.

**What completing this arc achieves:** convergence machinery demonstrated on five further independent real modules plus ground-truthed synthetic STEM content (known-answer recall, not just precision); the two governing directives causally attributed via factorial; the system's own live machinery (divergence, calibrator) reviewed by the system; a complete, defensible proof-of-concept dossier on which BR2, the paper, and any outreach stand. **What it does not give:** open-topology results, third-party STEM content at scale (BR2's job), production hardening, or validated domain specialists.

Per the discuss-before-proceeding discipline nothing above is implemented; the renumbering, the synth-tier compression, and the factorial-target proposal are all founder decisions.
